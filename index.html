01 · Smart Capture

Create reliable records with less typing

PDF and voice use the same safe workflow: provide the source, review the extracted information, correct it if needed, then approve record creation. One Smart Capture Page shows the full history, status, review, and conversion actions.

Capture 01

PDF Teaser → Property Draft

Upload an incoming Teaser and receive a saved, editable draft before a Property exists.

What the broker receives
  • Address, Activity, areas, pricing, Key Money, and availability
  • Descriptions and contact information when available
  • Missing or uncertain values clearly marked
  • Possible duplicate Property warning
  • One review screen before creating the Property
Concrete output A saved Property draft the broker can reopen, correct, and approve from the Smart Capture Page.
Capture 02

Voice → Property or Seeker Draft

Record a voice note in Salesforce and turn it into a reviewed CRM draft.

Broker experience
  • Mobile-priority recorder with desktop support
  • Record, stop, playback, re-record, and save
  • Automatic transcript linked to the voice note
  • Property or Seeker recognition
  • Editable extracted fields before record creation
Concrete output A transcript and editable Property or Seeker draft, reviewed and approved from the Smart Capture Page.
02 · Timely Follow-ups

Know who needs attention and why

Follow-ups are based on observable events: an unanswered Teaser, an agreed future contact date, or a relationship that has exceeded its contact cadence.

Follow-up 01

Unanswered Teaser Follow-up

Prompt the broker when a Seeker has not answered a specific Teaser.

Follow-up rule
  • Use the follow-up date recorded on the Teaser Log
  • If no date is set, follow up 14 days after sending
  • Close the action when a linked answer about the Property is received
  • Show six-month Teaser and response context
  • Prepare both email and WhatsApp drafts when available
Concrete output One due action containing the Property, send date, response history, and editable follow-up messages.
Follow-up 02

Agreed Future Contact

Remember when the broker and Seeker agreed to speak again.

How it works
  • Detect “call me in three months” and similar commitments
  • Schedule clear dates automatically
  • Propose an editable date when timing is vague
  • Link every reminder to the source conversation
  • Place the action in the responsible broker’s queue when due
Concrete output A scheduled follow-up containing the agreed timing, reason, source, and suggested message.
Follow-up 03

Relationship Priority & Contact Cadence

Let brokers decide how frequently each important Seeker should be contacted.

Broker-controlled cadence
  • High priority: remind after two months without two-way contact
  • Medium priority: remind after six months
  • Low priority: remind after twelve months
  • No inactivity reminder when no two-way contact has ever occurred
  • An explicit future-contact date takes priority over cadence
Concrete output A relationship-maintenance action with the last two-way conversation, recent outreach, and suggested reason to reconnect.
Follow-up 04

Evidence-backed Follow-up Ideas

Suggest relevant reasons to contact the Seeker based on prior conversations.

Ideas can include
  • Check whether a previously discussed transaction was completed
  • Confirm whether an older search remains active
  • Revisit an unresolved question or commitment
  • Discuss a Property or location already mentioned in the history
  • Ask for updated criteria when previous information may be stale
Concrete output Suggested talking points and editable messages, each linked to the communication that supports it.
Follow-up 05

Follow-up Email & WhatsApp Drafts

Generate send-ready messages directly from each follow-up reminder.

Draft behavior
  • Generate drafts directly from the follow-up reason and recent history
  • Use the language spoken in recent conversations
  • Prepare email and WhatsApp when both channels are available
  • Let the broker choose the shared sending number or email account
  • Require broker review and approval before sending
Concrete output Every follow-up reminder can produce two editable, channel-appropriate drafts ready for broker approval and sending.
03 · Seeker Recap

Prepare for the next conversation

The recap turns authorized communication, Teaser history, notes, meetings, and commitments into a concise preparation document.

Recap 01

On-demand Seeker Recap

Generate a pre-meeting summary covering the last six or twelve months.

Recap content
  • Current search and previously discussed locations
  • One shared history from all three WhatsApp numbers and both email accounts
  • Important meetings, calls, Tasks, and notes
  • Unique Properties presented and linked responses
  • Open commitments, questions, and unresolved outcomes
  • Suggested talking points linked to the relevant conversation
Concrete output A six- or twelve-month briefing with direct links to the original conversations and CRM records.
04 · Shared Workspaces

One general workspace, plus complete Seeker context

The Brokerage Intelligence Workspace contains the team’s Follow-up Queue. The same follow-ups also appear inside each Seeker Intelligence Workspace.

Workspace 01

Brokerage Intelligence Workspace

The shared team workspace for follow-ups and sent digest history.

General workspace
  • Today’s consolidated Seeker follow-up actions
  • One morning email digest per broker, defaulting to 08:00
  • History and delivery status of previously sent daily digests
  • Open, generate messages, complete, dismiss, or snooze
Concrete output One shared workspace showing who needs attention, why, and what the broker can do next.
Workspace 02

Seeker Intelligence Workspace

A dedicated section inside each Seeker record page answering what happened, what is due, and what to do next.

Workspace content
  • Relationship priority and contact dates
  • One timeline from all three WhatsApp numbers and both email accounts
  • The source number or email account shown for every message
  • Teasers, meetings, calls, notes, commitments, and open follow-ups
  • Six- or twelve-month recap generator
  • Message review and sending controls
Concrete output One Seeker page combining relationship context, pending work, communication history, recap, and message actions.
05 · New screens recap

Where each feature appears

Phase one adds a small set of connected screens rather than placing every feature on one page.

Brokerage Intelligence Workspace
Contains

Follow-up Queue and history and delivery status for Daily Email Digests.

Concrete example

“Eight follow-ups are due, and today’s email digest was delivered at 08:02.”

Follow-up Details
Contains

Opens from the Follow-up Queue or Seeker Intelligence Workspace. Shows why follow-up is due, relevant history, email and WhatsApp drafts, and available actions.

Concrete example

An unanswered Teaser and overdue commitment appear together, producing one coordinated email and WhatsApp message.

Seeker Intelligence Workspace
Contains

Priority, contact dates, all three WhatsApp and both email histories, Teasers, commitments, recap generator, and current Follow-up Action.

Concrete example

Before a meeting, the broker generates a twelve-month recap, checks unresolved topics, and opens the due follow-up.

Contains

One page for starting PDF or voice capture, reviewing draft history and status, correcting information, retrying failures, and creating a Property or Seeker.

Concrete example

A failed transcription is retried; another draft is corrected and approved as a Seeker without leaving the page.
